# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on the work, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or the publication of
private information. Technical disagreement is welcome when it remains
specific, evidence-based, and constructive.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[hello@edilec.com](mailto:hello@edilec.com). Do not use public issues for
sensitive reports. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate material and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect
the community.

Reports will be reviewed in context, with confidentiality preserved as far as
practical. Deliberately false or retaliatory reports are also unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, project discussions, and other
spaces where someone is representing the project or Edilec.
